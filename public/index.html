About Me

Hi! I am Danick Imholz. I study computer science at Rotterdam University of Applied Sciences. But programming is also a hobby of mine. I code in multiple languages, mainly Java, Javascript, and C++. The game engine you saw above is one of my projects. I have worked on the engine for a few years and also made a few prototype games in it.

C++ was the first programming language I ever used, but I didn't understand what I was doing. I was only 11 years old back then and I played a lot of Minecraft. This gave me the motivation to learn Java to make Minecraft plugins. But when I felt ready to make a plugin, I stopped playing Minecraft. And I wanted to make my own game instead. I tried a lot of engines, but all of them were not orientated around programmers and I had to learn a lot about the engine before I could start making games. That is why I made my own engine. If you want to see more of my projects please scroll further down or check my GitHub.
